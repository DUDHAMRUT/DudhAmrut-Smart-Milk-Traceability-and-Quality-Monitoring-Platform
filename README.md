# 🥛 DudhAmrut

Dudhamrut is an AI-based project designed to analyze milk quality and help identify whether milk is pure, adulterated, or unsafe for consumption.
The goal of this project is to promote food safety awareness and support better quality understanding using intelligent systems.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Dudhamrut applies artificial intelligence to a real-world dairy problem.
It focuses on making milk quality understanding simpler, faster, and more accessible.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

* Promote awareness about milk quality and food safety
* Show real-world application of Artificial Intelligence
* Support better understanding of dairy quality issues
* Encourage practical use of technology in daily life problems
